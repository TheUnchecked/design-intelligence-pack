# Brand Guidelines

## Purpose

This document defines the visual brand constraints to be applied by the Design Intelligence Pack.

When an official brand system is available, it takes precedence over generic visual recommendations.

The AI must not invent alternative colors, typography or visual identity when defined brand rules exist.

## Flame

Primary gradient:

`#FF5800 → #C80000`

### Gradient Rules

Use gradients intentionally.

They may support:

* visual emphasis;
* section differentiation;
* brand expression;
* key visual elements.

Do not apply gradients automatically to:

* every section;
* every card;
* every button;
* body text;
* decorative UI elements.

---

# 7. Color Usage

Color must communicate hierarchy, meaning or brand identity.

### Preferred hierarchy

1. Primary orange
2. Dark orange
3. Neutral grayscale
4. Secondary colors
5. Tertiary colors
6. Gradients where appropriate

Do not use every available brand color in the same composition unless the communication requires it.

---

# 8. Accessibility

Brand colors must not override accessibility requirements.

Verify:

* text contrast;
* background contrast;
* readability;
* non-color indicators;
* focus states;
* semantic meaning.

Do not communicate severity or status through color alone.

Always combine color with:

* labels;
* icons;
* position;
* text;
* patterns;
* or other visual indicators.

---

# 9. Presentation Design

For executive presentations:

* prioritize clarity over decoration;
* use the established typography hierarchy;
* maintain strong information hierarchy;
* use orange strategically for emphasis;
* use grayscale for supporting information;
* use secondary and tertiary colors semantically;
* avoid visual overload.

The presentation should remain recognizably aligned with the brand even when viewed without the logo.

---

# 10. AI Design Rules

When this document is available:

### MUST

* use the defined brand colors;
* use Segoe UI unless a documented exception exists;
* respect the defined typography hierarchy;
* preserve brand visual language;
* apply accessibility principles;
* use official gradients when gradients are appropriate.

### SHOULD

* prefer the simplest valid brand-compliant solution;
* use neutral colors for secondary information;
* reserve accent colors for meaningful emphasis;
* maintain consistent visual hierarchy.

### MUST NOT

* invent a new primary color;
* replace Segoe UI without justification;
* introduce unrelated visual styles;
* use arbitrary gradients;
* use all brand colors simultaneously without purpose;
* sacrifice readability for brand expression.

---

# 11. Brand Compliance Check

Before finalizing an artifact, verify:

### Typography

* Is the correct typeface used?
* Is hierarchy consistent?
* Are sizes appropriate for the medium?

### Color

* Are official colors being used?
* Is color serving a purpose?
* Is contrast sufficient?

### Gradients

* Are official gradients used?
* Is their application intentional?

### Consistency

* Does the artifact look like part of the same brand system?
* Are repeated elements consistent?

### Accessibility

* Is important information understandable without color?
* Are contrast and readability acceptable?

---

# Brand Principle

**The brand system is a constraint, not decoration.**

Design creativity should operate within the established identity while improving clarity, usability and communication.
